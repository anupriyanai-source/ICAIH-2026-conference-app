About ICAIH 2026

A premier platform for AI-driven healthcare transformation

ICAIH 2026 is the International Conference on Artificial Intelligence in Healthcare, taking place on 18 July 2026 at Anna Centenary Library, Chennai. The conference connects researchers, doctors, healthcare professionals, students, startups, innovators, policymakers, and industry leaders.

Read the complete International Conference on Artificial Intelligence in Healthcare overview.

The International Conference on Artificial Intelligence in Healthcare brings together healthcare professionals, researchers, policymakers, industry leaders, startups, innovators, and academic institutions to discuss the future of AI-enabled healthcare.

Artificial Intelligence is transforming healthcare by enabling faster diagnosis, predictive analytics, personalized treatment, and efficient healthcare delivery. By leveraging data, machine learning, and digital technologies, AI supports healthcare professionals in making informed decisions, improving patient outcomes, and expanding access to quality healthcare services.

Conference Focus Areas

AI applications across healthcare systems

AI for Public Health

Real-time data-driven insights, smart decisions, and better outcomes through disease surveillance, outbreak prediction, TB detection, and maternal health monitoring.

Impact: faster intervention, reduced mortality, improved population health outcomes.

AI for Rural Healthcare

AI-powered health kiosks, telemedicine, remote diagnostics, AI chatbots, community health support, and the G Care Health ATM model can connect villages to specialists.

AI in Diagnostics

AI assists radiology, pathology, ophthalmology, and cardiology through medical image analysis, tissue pattern detection, eye screening, ECG analysis, and risk prediction.

Benefits: faster diagnosis, improved accuracy, early disease detection.

Digital Public Health Infrastructure

Integration with Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission, electronic health records, health information exchange, and public health databases can support a unified digital healthcare ecosystem.

Healthcare AI Startups

Emerging innovation areas include medical imaging AI, health analytics, drug discovery, AI-powered telemedicine, and personalized healthcare.

AI Skills and Workforce Development

Future healthcare requires AI doctors, AI nurses, health data scientists, biomedical AI engineers, and AI public health experts. The proposal is a Tamil Nadu Centre for AI in Healthcare Excellence.

ICAIH 2026 Website User Guidelines

How to Apply for Competitions, Research Paper Submission, Awards, Sponsorship, and Stall Booking

Welcome to ICAIH 2026 – International Conference on Artificial Intelligence in Healthcare. The website provides separate forms for competitions, research paper submissions, award nominations, sponsorship enquiries, and stall/exhibitor bookings. Please read the instructions below before submitting any form.

🏆

1. Competition Application Guidelines

Use this form if you want to participate in any ICAIH 2026 pre-conference competition.

Steps to Apply

  1. Go to the website header menu and click Competition.
  2. Select Pre-Conference Competitions Application Form.
  3. Fill in your personal details such as name, email ID, mobile number, WhatsApp number, and city/state.
  4. Enter your institution or organization details.
  5. Select your participant category, such as School Student, UG Student, PG Student, Medical Student, Research Scholar, Faculty, Startup/Innovator, or Other.
  6. Choose the competition category: AI in Healthcare Innovation Challenge, Research Paper / Poster Presentation Competition, Healthcare Case Study Challenge, or Ideathon – Healthcare 2035.
  7. Select whether you are participating as an Individual or as a Team.
  8. Enter your topic or theme area, short description, and expected healthcare impact.
  9. Read and accept the declaration statements.
  10. Enter the applicant or team leader name, date, and typed signature, then click Submit Competition Application.

Supporting Document Instructions

File upload is not required in the website form. After submitting the form, send documents such as proposal, abstract, poster, PPT, case study, ID proof, or supporting document by email.

Send to: divyav16.ai@gmail.com

File naming format: YourName_Competition_Topic.pdf / docx / pptx

Example: Pavithra_Competition_Telemedicine.pdf

📄

2. Research Paper Submission Guidelines

Use this form if you want to submit a research paper, poster, case study, review paper, innovation project, or startup solution for ICAIH 2026.

Steps to Submit

  1. Go to the website header menu and click Competition.
  2. Select Research Paper Submission Form.
  3. Fill in your personal, academic, and professional details.
  4. Select the applicant category, presentation type, and conference theme or topic area.
  5. Enter the abstract or short summary, keywords, author details, co-authors, and guide or mentor if applicable.
  6. Select your preferred presentation mode and confirm whether you will attend the conference in person.
  7. Read and accept the declarations, enter name, date, and typed signature, then submit the form.

Supporting Document Instructions

File upload is not required in the website form. After submitting the form, send your research paper or supporting document by email.

Send to: divyav16.ai@gmail.com

File naming format: YourName_Research_Topic.pdf / docx

Example: Pavithra_AI_Diagnosis_Paper.pdf

🎖

3. International Awards Nomination Guidelines

Use this form if you want to apply for or nominate someone for an ICAIH 2026 International Award.

Steps to Submit an Award Nomination

  1. Go to the website header menu and click Competition.
  2. Select ICAIH 2026 International Awards Nomination Form.
  3. Fill in nominee details such as name, email ID, mobile number, country, and LinkedIn profile or website.
  4. Enter professional or organization details and select the appropriate award category.
  5. Write a short biography, key achievements, research publications, patents, previous awards, and award justification if applicable.
  6. Select the supporting documents you will send by email.
  7. Read and accept the declaration, enter applicant name, date, and typed signature, then click Submit Award Nomination.

Supporting Document Instructions

File upload is not required in the website form. After submitting the award nomination form, send your CV/resume, photograph, research papers, patent documents, project details, certificates, or other supporting documents by email.

Send to: divyav16.ai@gmail.com

File naming format: YourName_Award_Category.pdf / docx / jpg

Example: Pavithra_Young_Researcher_Award.pdf

🤝

4. Sponsorship Guidelines

Use this section if your company, hospital, institution, startup, or organization wants to sponsor ICAIH 2026.

  1. Go to the website header menu and click Sponsorship.
  2. Review the available sponsorship categories and benefits.
  3. Click Become a Sponsor and fill in company name, contact person, email ID, phone number, and sponsorship tier.
  4. The selected sponsor fee will be displayed automatically after choosing the tier.
  5. Add specific requirements in the message box if needed.
  6. Use the payment option or QR code if you want to proceed with sponsor payment, then click Submit Sponsor Inquiry.

Available options may include Title Sponsor, Co-Title Sponsor, Healthcare Innovation Sponsor, Platinum Sponsor, AI Transformation Sponsor, Gold Sponsor, Delegate Kit Sponsor, Knowledge Partner, Technology Partner, Healthcare Partner, Silver Sponsor, and Associate Sponsor.

🏪

5. Stall / Exhibitor Booking Guidelines

Use the dedicated Stall Booking option if you want to book a stall, pavilion, or exhibitor space at ICAIH 2026.

  1. Go to the website header menu and click Sponsorship.
  2. Click Book Stall Space.
  3. Fill in company name, contact person name, email ID, and phone number.
  4. Open the Stall / Exhibitor Tier dropdown and select Premium Exhibitor, Standard Exhibitor, or Standard Pavilion.
  5. Add stall-related requirements such as space, branding, or product display details.
  6. Use the payment option or QR code if required, then click Submit Sponsor Inquiry.
✉

6. Important Instructions for All Applicants

  • All required fields marked with * must be completed.
  • Your email ID and phone number must be correct.
  • Your form details must be accurate before submission.
  • Supporting documents must be sent separately by email after form submission.
  • The file name must follow the required format.
  • Keep a copy of your submitted details and sent documents for future reference.
