About ICAIH 2026

The International Conference on Artificial Intelligence in Healthcare brings together doctors, healthcare professionals, researchers, students, startups, policymakers, technology companies, and academic institutions. The conference focuses on responsible, practical, and inclusive uses of AI across healthcare delivery, diagnostics, public health, research, education, and patient care.

The event will be held on 18 July 2026 at Anna Centenary Library, Kotturpuram, Chennai.

Conference Focus Areas

  • AI-assisted diagnosis and medical imaging
  • Predictive analytics and personalized treatment
  • Telemedicine, remote monitoring, and digital health
  • Healthcare robotics and intelligent automation
  • Responsible AI, privacy, ethics, and governance
  • Public-health intelligence and inclusive healthcare access
  • Startup innovation, research commercialization, and industry collaboration

Who Should Attend?

ICAIH 2026 is suitable for medical professionals, healthcare administrators, researchers, academicians, students, research scholars, startup founders, innovators, industry experts, technology companies, hospitals, colleges, and public-sector representatives.

Research, Competitions, Awards, and Exhibitions

Participants can explore keynote sessions, research-paper presentations, pre-conference competitions, healthcare innovation exhibitions, award nominations, sponsorship opportunities, stall bookings, and professional networking.
